## Summary

Data scientist and aerospace engineer with nearly four decades of experience in
numerical modeling, orbit determination, and large-scale data analysis. Career
spans roughly 25 years at the University of Texas at Austin — from NASA Earth
Science software development, including Hubble Space Telescope astrometry, to
precision orbit determination and space geodesy at the Center for Space Research
— followed by data science roles across sensing, telecommunications, and energy
research in Germany.
