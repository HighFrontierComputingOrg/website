About The Author

The High Frontier Computing 1958 to 1982 project is being developed as a potential UT Austin dissertation.

The Author

Noah Smith

This project is not a detached survey. Its author spent a quarter-century at the University of Texas at Austin — first as a software developer on NASA Earth Science work, including Hubble Space Telescope astrometry, then as an aerospace engineer at the Center for Space Research. That is the same institution, and much the same lineage of machines and methods, that the essay traces from the CDC 6600 to UTOPIA.

The through-line of a career — numerical modeling, orbit determination, and large-scale data — is the through-line of the history itself. High Frontier Computing is an attempt to preserve that world of code, machines, and mathematics before it fades from living memory.
